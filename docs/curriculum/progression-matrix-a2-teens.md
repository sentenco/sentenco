# A2 Teens — Intra-Level Progression Matrix (Pilot)

Per the Sentenco Intra-Level Progression Framework (Perplexity, 2026-07-08):
three tiers of four units, six progression levers, same 9-part lesson
architecture throughout — only the language/task load changes.

| Unit | Tier | Topic | Grammar load | Text length target | Vocabulary type | Scaffolding | Cognitive demand | Skill integration |
|------|------|-------|---------------|---------------------|------------------|--------------|-------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | Foundation | Identity and Personal Style | 1 target: simple present for description | 3–5 short sentences | concrete (style, appearance nouns) | heavy: word banks, sentence frames | describe | isolated |
| 2 | Foundation | Friends and Social Life | 1 target: simple present + can | 3–5 short sentences | concrete (personality nouns) | heavy | describe | isolated |
| 3 | Foundation | School and Study Skills | 1 target: frequency adverbs (usually/sometimes) | 3–5 short sentences | concrete (school routine) | heavy | describe | isolated |
| 4 | Foundation | Free Time and Hobbies | 1 target: simple past (regular verbs) | 3–5 short sentences | concrete (hobby nouns/verbs) | heavy | describe | isolated |
| 5 | Combination | Media and Technology | 2 targets: simple past + comparatives | 6–8 sentence paragraph | concrete + opinion adjectives | medium: partial prompts | compare / give reasons | some integration (read→speak) |
| 6 | Combination | Food and Going Out | 2 targets: comparatives + because | 6–8 sentence paragraph | concrete + opinion adjectives | medium | compare / give reasons | some integration (read→speak) |
| 7 | Combination | Town Life and Getting Around | 2 targets: simple past + connectors (and/but) | 6–8 sentence paragraph | concrete + opinion adjectives | medium | compare / give reasons | some integration (listen→write) |
| 8 | Combination | Travel and Holidays | 2 targets: simple past + comparatives | 6–8 sentence paragraph | concrete + opinion adjectives | medium | compare / give reasons | some integration (read→speak) |
| 9 | Bridge | Health and Fitness | 2–3 targets: simple past + comparative + because, combined in one text | 8–12+ sentence connected text | more abstract (habits, wellbeing) | light: prompts/checklist only | justify / weigh options | integrated (compare→discuss→write) |
| 10 | Bridge | Entertainment and Events | 2–3 targets: simple past + comparative + going to | 8–12+ sentence connected text | more abstract (opinion, recommendation) | light | justify / explain | integrated (compare→discuss→write) |
| 11 | Bridge | Future Plans and Goals | 2–3 targets: going to + used to + because | 8–12+ sentence connected text | more abstract (goals, ambition, change) | light | justify / explain consequences | integrated (reflect→speak→write) |
| 12 | Bridge | Projects, Events, and Final Showcase | 2–3 targets integrated: all of the above combined | 8–12+ sentence connected text | more abstract (progress, reflection) | minimal: prompts/output criteria only | justify / reflect / synthesize | fully integrated (research→plan→reflect→write) |

**Review lessons (Lesson 5 of every unit)** stay tier-matched to their unit —
Unit 4's review is still foundation-level, Unit 8's review is
combination-level, Unit 12's review is bridge-level — so pacing doesn't
flatten out at exactly the moments meant to consolidate each tier.

**Self-containment preserved:** every lesson still opens with warm-up +
model text before recycling into practice, so a student starting at
Unit 9 isn't lost — the harder load comes from combined grammar,
longer text, and less scaffolding within that unit, not from assuming
knowledge of Units 1–8's specific vocabulary.
