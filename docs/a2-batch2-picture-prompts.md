## Shared style (add to every prompt)

Cute kawaii chibi cartoon illustration, thin dark-brown outlines, soft flat shading, bright friendly colors. Plain white background, no text, no letters, no watermark. Square 1:1, subject centered and filling most of the frame. Any person has a natural light skin tone (no orange, yellow or peach tint); vary people by hair, clothes and features. Everything gentle and friendly, nothing scary.
