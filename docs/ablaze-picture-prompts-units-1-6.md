# Ablaze A2 (Teens) picture prompts, Units 1 to 6

New pictures for the rebuilt Ablaze A2 Units 1 to 6. Save each as `.jpeg` at the path shown, under `public/curriculum/ignite/chibi/`. A missing picture shows a dashed placeholder in the lesson, so they can arrive in any order.

These lessons also reuse pictures from the Ignite A1 list (clothes, free time, hobbies, tech, food, meals, school day). Those are in `docs/ignite-all-picture-prompts.md`, so generate that list first or together.

Cast portraits are older teens (13 to 17) and each unit has its own 4 friends.
