## 3. One-page tester guide (attach or paste after the invite)

Sentenco beta: what to try

1. Sign up and set up your profile (account menu, Edit profile). Add a photo and crop it.
2. Homeroom: write a post, like or comment on someone else's, and search for a teacher by name to open their profile.
3. Teacher's Desk: try the Spin the Wheel, the Digital Notebook, Lesson Feedback and the Syllabus Generator.
4. Curriculum: open an A1 or A2 lesson and teach it, or read through it as if you would.
5. Speaking, Grammar, Vocabulary and Writing: open two or three activities and run one with a real or pretend student.
6. Reading: open a story. Try a different level.
7. Articles: read one and try its activities.
8. Custom Lessons: send a request, and if you have a lesson of your own, try Share to post it in Homeroom.
9. Phone check: open Sentenco on your phone and tell us what looks wrong.

When something goes wrong, tell us:
- What you were doing (which lesson or page).
- What you expected, and what happened instead.
- A screenshot if you can.
- Your device and browser (for example, iPhone Safari, or Windows Chrome).

Questions we'd love answered:
- Could you find what you wanted without help?
- Was anything confusing, or too slow?
- Would you use this with your students next week? What's stopping you?
- What's the one thing you wish it had?

Send everything to hello@sentenco.com. Thank you!
